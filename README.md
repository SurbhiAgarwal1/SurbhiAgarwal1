## 👩‍💻 About Me

Hi, I'm **Surbhi Agarwal**. I'm a Computer Science (AI/ML) undergraduate who is deeply curious about how real software systems are built, evolve, and improve over time. I enjoy exploring unfamiliar codebases, understanding the reasoning behind existing implementations, and gradually turning that understanding into thoughtful improvements.

What excites me most about technology is the constant opportunity to discover something new — a different way of approaching a problem, a tool that changes how systems are built, or a project that challenges what I already know. I'm naturally drawn to experimenting with ideas, exploring emerging technologies, and learning by working on real projects.

One quality that defines how I approach development is **curiosity**. I'm comfortable stepping into areas I don't fully understand yet and learning by breaking things down, asking questions, and figuring out how systems actually work. Many of the most valuable lessons I've learned as a developer have come from exploring real projects and observing how experienced engineers approach complex problems.

I appreciate well-designed systems, clear thinking, and the quiet satisfaction of making something work a little better than it did before. I strongly believe that the work behind a repository often speaks more clearly than any introduction, and I try to let my projects reflect the way I think, learn, and build.
