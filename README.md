## 🙋‍♀️ About Me

I'm **Surbhi Agarwal**, a CS (AI/ML) undergraduate curious about how real software systems are built, evolve, and improve. I enjoy exploring unfamiliar codebases, understanding the *why* behind implementations, and turning that understanding into thoughtful improvements.

I'm drawn to **emerging technologies**, real-world problems, and projects that challenge what I already know. **Curiosity** is at the core of how I work — I step into the unknown, break things down, ask questions, and figure out how systems actually work.

I appreciate well-designed systems, clear thinking, and the quiet satisfaction of making something work a little better than before.

> 💬 *I strongly believe the work behind a repository speaks more clearly than any introduction.*
